 # Jesse Pruitt Astrophotography Gallery
## This will be a space dedicated to displaying my astrophotography projects and the beautiful details of our local galactic features.

### I have always been interested in astronomy and fascinated by the night sky. A few years ago I developed a disability that caused me to have to step back from my art career and look for other avenues to fit my new limitations. I turned to astrophotography as it allows me to set-up a a telescope and camera in my backyard and more or less automate photographing distant objects. It is my goal to share beautiful pictures of deep sky objects as well as knowledge of our local neighborhood and passion for diy space exploration.
