Digital Rights

Understanding and protecting creative work in the age of artificial intelligence.

Know Your Rights

Creative professionals have legal and ethical rights concerning ownership, attribution, compensation, privacy, and control of their work. Understanding these rights helps creators navigate rapidly changing technologies while protecting their contributions.

Key Rights for Creative Professionals

Copyright Protection

Copyright law protects original creative works and provides creators with control over how their work is reproduced and distributed.

Attribution Rights

Artists deserve recognition and proper credit for their creative contributions.

Fair Compensation

Creative work has value, and professionals should be compensated fairly for its use.

Privacy Rights

Individuals have rights regarding the collection, storage, and use of personal information.

Ownership and AI

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly integrated into creative industries, questions surrounding ownership, licensing, training data, and attribution continue to evolve. Remaining informed helps creators advocate for fair and transparent practices.

Common Concerns

Unauthorized Use

Creative works may be used without permission, proper licensing, or payment.

Lack of Attribution

Creators may lose visibility and recognition when work is used without credit.

Training Data Transparency

Artists increasingly seek transparency regarding how creative works are collected and used for AI development.
