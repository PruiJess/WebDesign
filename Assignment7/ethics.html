Ethical AI for Employers

Building a future where technology supports creativity instead of replacing it.

Partnering for a Creative Future

Employers play an important role in shaping how artificial intelligence is used in creative industries. Responsible implementation helps organizations innovate while continuing to value human creativity, expertise, and judgment.

Key Principles

Transparency

Clearly communicate when and how AI tools are being used.

Fair Attribution

Respect and acknowledge the contributions of human creators.

Human Oversight

Keep people involved in important creative and business decisions.

Support Workers

Provide training and opportunities for employees affected by new technologies.

Best Practices Checklist

  • Be transparent about AI use
  • Respect copyright and licensing
  • Maintain human oversight
  • Provide employee training
  • Support workforce development
  • Follow legal and industry standards
