Ethical Artificial Intelligence

Building technology that supports people, creativity, and transparency.

Why Ethics Matter

Responsible AI development requires transparency, accountability, fairness, and respect for creators whose work contributes to innovation.

Core Ethical Principles

Transparency

Organizations should clearly communicate how AI systems are trained and used.

Accountability

Decisions made using AI should remain subject to human oversight.

Fairness

Systems should avoid bias and treat creators equitably.

Respect for Creators

Human creative work should be valued, attributed, and protected.

Responsible Innovation

New technologies can provide benefits while still supporting the people whose creativity, experience, and expertise help make them possible.

Ethical Questions

Consent

Should creators be able to opt in or opt out of training datasets?

Compensation

How should creators be compensated when their work contributes to AI systems?

Attribution

How can creative contributions remain visible in an automated environment?
